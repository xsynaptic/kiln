# Detached contract

A brief handed you this task and no human is watching. Read the [envelope contract](./envelope.md) first. You open the PR and stop; the gatekeeper lands it. The PR is your only channel to a human, so put blockers and human decisions there rather than guessing.

## Your bead

Dispatch claimed it; the brief names it. Read it with `bd show <id>`. Your write grant is comments on it; dispatch settles its status, assignee and gates from your receipt.

## Your gate

`kiln-gate author`, once, over the committed tree you deliver.

## The structured output

Your final turn fills a structured output whose schema describes each field; dispatch acts on it. Report in prose as well.

## Budget notices

A message naming your context or elapsed time is from the harness, outranks your own estimate, and comes once. Answer it by delivering or by handing off. A handoff is: commit what passes `kiln-gate author`, push, open the PR saying what remains, and comment on your bead with what you did, what you left, what the brief had wrong, and the branch.
