# Worker playbook

Read the [shared playbook](./shared.md) first.

## Amendments

A bead's criteria can be false by the time you build on them. Verify what you lean on. When one fails, comment on the bead with the criterion as filed, the evidence against it, and what you did instead. The gatekeeper grades against amendments that carry evidence.

## Review, then close

Run the `critique` skill before your gate. A finding it would have you propose to a human is yours to call: adopt it, or note the rejection in the PR body.

## Handing off

Hand off when the work won't fit the room left, rather than half-finishing it. Skip the critique on a handoff.
